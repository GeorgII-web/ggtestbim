# ggtestbim

Personal 3D game in Rust on [Bevy](https://bevyengine.org) 0.20, target Windows x86_64.

## Code

| Module | What |
|---|---|
| `src/main.rs` | app, the directional light (moved by `daylight`), sky and ambient defaults, plugin list |
| `src/level.rs` | endless world streamed in 60 m chunks (2×2 blocks): chunks within 3 of the player are generated (2 per frame, nearest first), chunks beyond 4 are dropped, a revisited area is generated anew with a fresh seed. Each chunk has a random theme (downtown, hills, parkland, mixed; each with a tower chance and a pit chance): terraces with stairs, buildings (a rectangular core with rooms, doors, windows, a switchback stairwell to a walkable roof; 65 % get a smaller core with room for up to 2 `Wing`s on sides without passages, giving L / T / U / cross shapes: one open room per floor, windows, a street door, a door into the core per floor and one from the core onto the wing roof; towers of 10–18 floors), plazas, parks, pits (at most one per chunk, on street level, `PitPlan`: a kind from `PIT_KINDS` — narrow deep shaft 9–13 m / 16–32 m, wide shallow crater 16–20 m / 4–8 m, pit 12–20 m / 8–22 m — at a random place in the block, depth cut to what the stairs can reach; built in a local frame that `swap` / `flip_x` / `flip_z` turn, so the stairs start in any corner and turn either way; the ground is cut around the hole, retaining walls, steps spiral down along the walls with corner landings and a railing (none on the last 3 steps), a rim railing open at the stairs' top; the floor inside the stairs is split in four quarters with a `Feature` each — pillars, a ruined corner, a 0.5 m platform, a two-tier mound, a pool, a boulder or nothing — keeping 1.2 m walkways; 1–4 artifacts over the middle of some quarters, on top of a platform or mound; `Level::new_artifacts`), corridors between upper floors and bridges between roofs. Static parts are axis-aligned boxes: collider per chunk (`Level::near`) and, merged per material, one mesh with world-space UVs. Trees and benches are props (own entities, `PropBoxes` colliders) that change while behind the player (`haunt`). Materials: 10 fixed plus 4 floor and 16 wall styles random per world. Watcher spots (`Spot`: position, kind, several escapes of waypoints): behind windows of every floor, at building corners, one roof corner per building; escapes that hit a box are dropped, and spots left without one. `Level::blocked` is a segment line-of-sight test against the boxes. Indoor volumes (inside a building's outer walls up to the roof, covered corridors): faces in them use an indoor copy of their material with a 1×1 occlusion map, so they get only `INDOOR_AMBIENT` (15 %) of the ambient light plus a faint textured glow (`INDOOR_GLOW`, 15 nits × color, `emissive_exposure_weight` 1 so the camera exposure scales it; with the default 0 it is raw HDR and rooms glare) so rooms are never fully dark and rooms are lit by sun through windows and doors. N drops the world |
| `src/textures.rs` | procedural tileable grayscale textures, varied by a seed (sizes, counts, direction), with a mip chain, tinted by the material color; wall and floor kinds for the random styles |
| `src/player.rs` | first-person walker (on a rope, `Ride`: hangs 1.9 m below it, W / S along it toward / away from the view at 5 m/s, blocked by boxes, Space lets go, at the far end climbs onto the tied surface, over a parapet or ledge up to 2.5 m above or onto a floor in front, else lets go; test: walking down a 20 m pit): mouse look, instant direction on the ground and a 0.25 s glide after release, run 12 m/s by default, Shift walk 3 m/s, Ctrl crouch (0.9 m, fits a window), jump (apex 0.94 m; crouched 1.12 m, onto a 1.5 m terrace ledge) with coyote time and buffer, gravity, box collision against chunks, cars and props with step-up (also in the air, onto sills), snap-down on stairs, landing dip, distance fog; respawn below -30 m or on N; health 100 (`hurt`, `respawn`, `dead` blocks movement, look and shooting; `since_spawn` for the dogs' grace), landing faster than 13 m/s hurts 7 per m/s above. Tests: climbing a 6-story stairwell and a terrace stair, crouch jumps through a window and onto a terrace ledge; level: no coplanar visible faces, watcher escapes clear |
| `src/cars.rs` | 3 cars per new chunk on street lanes, driving straight, braking for the player ahead, removed in unloaded chunks; `CarBoxes` colliders |
| `src/watchers.rs` | strangers: tall dark figures with a pale face and glowing eyes. A director keeps up to 5 around the player: every 1.5–4 s one appears hidden at the cover (escape end out of the player's line of sight) of a spot 18–80 m away (windows 50 %, corners 40 %, roofs 10 %), creeps out (1.6 m/s) to the spot and watches; seen 0.4–1.2 s, approached within 16 m, or after 5–14 s it runs back (8 m/s) along its escape (or another that ends hidden) and is removed once the view is blocked; if its cover is in view it ducks (shrinks down) instead of standing; within 3 m it ducks at once. While it sees the player (line of sight within `Exposure`) it sets `DogCall` with `seen` (it guides the dogs to the player's exact place); a `Noises` event it hears sets it without (dogs near it come to the noise). A bullet kills it: falls backwards, eyes out, sinks |
| `src/clouds.rs` | 45 random sphere clouds 90–140 m up, drifting with the wind, recycled with a new shape on the far side when out of range; color follows the time of day (white, orange at dusk and dawn, dark slate at night) |
| `src/optic.rs` | rifle-scope optic: right click on/off (eases in to the last zoom, first 10x), smooth wheel zoom 2–30x, FOV and mouse look scaled by the zoom, overlay (lens square and black side bars placed in pixels from the window size, no gaps) with a generated reticle texture (black outside the lens, rim shade, crosshair with posts and mil dots) under the HUD, zoom label |
| `src/weapon.rs` | pistol: first-person model (child of the camera, no shadows), crosshair (dot and four ticks; the gap is the next shot's spread cone on screen, from the same `spread` as the shot, eased), ammo bottom right; left click (mouse grabbed) shoots inside a random cone around the view (radius 0.3° still, 1.2° walking, 3.5° running, 5° in the air, times 1 / 1.6 / 2.3 / 3 for the 1st / 2nd / 3rd / later shot of a quick series; the series cools by one shot per 0.35 s; `Player::motion`) via `Level::raycast` / `ray_box` (cars and props too): muzzle flash with a short point light, recoil, bullet mark on level boxes (thin dark square, 60 s, shrinks the last 3 s, max 300, removed with its chunk) and 9 sparks; hits on figures kill them and spray dark red bits; empty: slide locked back, a click is a dry-fire twitch; sounds through `SoundQueue`; 12 rounds, R reloads (1.3 s: cant, magazine out down the grip, new one in, slide racked, back); pistol and crosshair hidden behind the optic |
| `src/render_scale.rs` | render resolution: the 3D camera draws into an image 720 px high (width from the window's aspect; F cycles 720 / 900 / 1080 / native, never above the window), a 2D window camera (`IsDefaultUiCamera`) stretches it under the full-size UI; the HUD shows the height next to the fps |
| `src/sound.rs` | all sounds synthesized at startup (custom `Synth` audio source, no files): footsteps by distance walked (stride 0.8 m walk, 1.9 m run; volume 0.1 walk, 0.18 run, 0.04 crouched, 0.3 landing), dog paws (spatial, stride 0.45 m walking / 0.8 m running); `Noises`: how far the player's steps carry (crouch 3 m, walk 8, run 22, landing 14) and shots (90 m), heard by dogs and strangers, shot, dry fire, reload clicks timed to the animation, bullet hits (one with a ricochet whine), a figure's death cry, car engine loop (cars within 40 m, pitch by speed), dog barks, bite, yelp, player grunt, flashlight click; other modules queue one-shots in `SoundQueue`; spatial sounds use scale 0.12 (rodio 1/d² falloff), listener on the camera |
| `src/daylight.rs` | day and night (`sun_height`, used by clouds and the flashlight's exposure): 24 h in 20 min, random start hour on launch and on N; one directional light is the sun (east at 6:00, warm when low) or the moon (dim, bluish); sky / fog / ambient (300 day, 25 night) follow; sun and moon discs (unlit spheres 400 m from the camera along the light directions, no fog, hidden below the horizon, the sun orange when low); clock under the fps |
| `src/targets.rs` | shared target components: `Shootable` (kind, body box), `Killed` (death timer, set by the weapon), `Blip` (radar) |
| `src/dogs.rs` | stray dogs in packs of 3–4 (up to 2 packs), spawned out of sight on a street 45–80 m away, removed beyond 120 m; none in the first 30 s after a (re)spawn or new world. A pack wanders together (cohesion 6 m, spacing 1.2 m); when one dog sees the player (`Exposure`), hears a `Noises` event or gets a `DogCall`, the whole pack barks and chases (9.5 m/s); a stranger's guidance (`DogCall` with `seen`) reaches every dog within 120 m: for 4 s after its last sight the dogs know the player's exact place and search a path to it at once, again every 2 s or when the player moved 3 m (unless they can run straight at the player): straight at a visible player, else sniffs the player's `Trail` (breadcrumbs every 1.5 m, 600 kept, smelled for 60 s by every dog; from the nearest visible crumb, else the nearest one smelled behind walls with a path search to it, toward the newest, also into buildings and up stairs), else to the last seen/heard place; gives up after 15 s without a sight, sound or fresh crumb, at the end of a cold trail it wanders. Blocked, no progress (0.7 m in 1.5 s) or the target above / below it (roof, upper floor, pit): searches a path (`find_path`: weighted A* over a 0.7 m walkable grid, sub-steps of 0.35 m with three ground rays so stair edges are never missed, steps to 0.6 m, drops to 3 m, 32 m around and 70 m up / down, 8000 nodes, one search per frame for all dogs; a path that does not reach the target is taken only if it gets the dog 3 m closer and does not end at one of its last 4 dead ends; else the dog remembers the place as a dead end, runs to a random place 15–30 m away (`explore`) and searches again from there) and follows its waypoints (dropped after 10 s or when the straight way is clear); never stuck: wanting to move but within 1 m for 6 s, or 2 failed searches, puts it on a random street 30–70 m away out of the player's sight. Player on a rope: chasing dogs search a path to the rope end the player is nearer to (ground under the throw point, or under / on the tied surface), wait there and bite by jumping up to 2.4 m. Tests: paths from the street to a 3-story roof, into a 20 m pit and to every artifact of 60 random pits. Every dog has random `Traits`: fur color (7 bases, varied), strength 0.5–1.8 (bite damage factor; size 0.75–1.15, also its hit box), speed factor 0.75–1.3, smartness 0–1 (stuck check 1.2–2.7 s, path search reach 16–35 m and 30–100 % of the nodes, guided search every 2–4 s). Bites within 1.3 m (12 every 0.9 s); walks on the level boxes (ground raycast, steps to 0.6 m, drops to 3 m); red glowing eyes; trotting legs; paw sounds; killable |
| `src/health.rs` | health bar bottom left with value, red flash and grunt on damage, regeneration 2/s after 6 s without damage, death screen 3 s then respawn at the spawn point |
| `src/radar.rs` | radar bottom left above the health bar: turns with the view, 60 m range, red dogs, pale strangers |
| `src/flashlight.rs` | flashlight on F (spot light on the camera, 4 Mlm, 90 m, no shadows); `Exposure`: how far strangers and dogs see the player (day 50 m, night 22 m, flashlight on ≥ 70 m, crouched ×0.7) |
| `src/lightsticks.rs` | 4 throws a light stick (13 m/s plus a lift, gravity, bounces off walls, rests on the first floor), green point light 120 klm / 18 m for 300 s (fading the last 10 s), at most 10 |
| `src/ropes.rs` | E throws a rope at the surface in the view (60 m, line from the hands clear, at least 3 m): tied there, the other end in the air above the player's head, the player takes hold at once; E lets go; the rope disappears as soon as the player is off it (let go, climbed off, died, N) |
| `src/artifacts.rs` | anomaly artifacts at pit bottoms: violet glowing octahedra that float, turn and pulse with a point light (on within 45 m), taken by walking into them (1.4 m), count under the clock; removed with their chunk and on N |
| `src/hud.rs` | top line: `~ help` at the left (~ unfolds the controls), compass strip (S in red) with the heading right of it at the center, fps at the right |

Geometry rules the collider relies on: walls, slabs and steps are boxes; a stair step rises 0.25 m (under the 0.55 m
step-up); stair flights never lie inside another flight's head room (switchback lanes); slabs are at least 0.2 m thick (above the 0.15 m substep); no two boxes share a visible face plane
(slabs stop at the inner wall face, interior walls stop under the slab above, stair railings sit inside the hole's
lane, plaza low walls differ in height, benches keep apart), or the faces flicker; the test
`level::tests::no_coplanar_visible_faces` checks this on 20 generated chunks (props left out).

## Build

- The repo lives in WSL; the game is cross-built to a Windows exe with MinGW (no Windows Rust toolchain needed).
- `scripts/cargo-gnu.sh build [--release]` → `target/x86_64-pc-windows-gnu/<profile>/ggtestbim.exe`.
- `scripts/run-win.sh [--release]` builds, copies the exe (and `assets/`) to `C:\dev\ggtestbim` and starts it.
- Plain `cargo build` (Linux) needs `pkg-config`, `libudev-dev`, `libasound2-dev`, `libwayland-dev`; not set up.
- Gates before a commit: `cargo fmt --check`, `scripts/cargo-gnu.sh clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`, and the
  tests: `scripts/cargo-gnu.sh test --no-run`, copy the printed test exe to a Windows folder (`C:\dev\ggtestbim-test`)
  and run it there (Linux can't build Bevy here; the exe needs Windows).

## Performance

The target machine has an integrated Intel Iris Xe GPU (Vulkan): the cost is per pixel, not geometry (a chunk is ~730
boxes). MSAA is off, shadows are 2 cascades to 50 m (the first to 6 m, against saw-tooth shadow edges up close) on a 1024 map with the `Hardware2x2` filter, textures use 4×
anisotropy, 5×5 chunks are loaded with fog at 45–115 m; that measured 50–65 fps (vsync 60), against ~22 fps with
MSAA 4×, 3–4 cascades and 7×7 chunks. G toggles shadows in game; the HUD shows fps. The window is high-DPI (about 2900×1700 physical): the 3D scene renders at 720p by default whatever the window or full-screen size (about a fifth of the pixels; F2 cycles the resolution). Measure before raising any of these.
